Logo concepts

Five directions for the Receipts mark, next to the current one. Each is shown as an app icon (light, dark, accent), at favicon sizes and with the wordmark. Switch themes to see it in every palette.

All marks are plain SVG built on the same 64×64 grid and coloured by the theme tokens, so the chosen one can drop straight into the favicon, the home-screen icon and the logo button in demo.html and index.html. The stamp and £ concepts use JetBrains Mono for their lettering; for a final asset those letters would be outlined to paths.

Questions

Fair questions.

What if I can't afford it?

Stakes are opt-in and capped daily, so one bad night never becomes a bad month. The Free plan has no stakes at all, just the receipt.

Which charities does the money go to?

You pick a cause: mental health, education or climate. We'll announce our charity partners at launch, and 100% of every £1 is passed on.

Can Receipts see what I watch?

No. Receipts only sees how long each app was open. Never what you watched, posted or messaged.

Can't I just delete the app?

Sure. But you set the stakes because future you asked present you to. And with a friend pot, your mates will notice.
